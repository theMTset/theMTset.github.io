Why Hawaii

This isn’t a place I chose from a map.

I’ve lived in Hawaii before. I spent six months doing farm work trades on Kauai and most of my time in the islands on the Big Island. One of my sons was born there.

I know island life asks for adaptability, respect for the land and local community, and a willingness to contribute. Kauai and the Big Island are familiar to me, but I’m open to any island. The chance to experience a part of Hawaii I haven’t known before is part of what makes Workaway appealing.

This return is part of a considered life transition. I hope to contribute somewhere worthwhile while I work toward a stable long-term home where my children can spend meaningful time with me.

Living + working together

Clear, capable, and easy to work beside.

I’m used to being responsible for my own work, but I don’t disappear into a project and make assumptions. I ask questions up front, communicate when something changes, and say plainly when a task is outside my experience.

I take care of tools and shared spaces. I’m comfortable working independently or with other people, and I don’t need to be entertained during my time off. Curiosity has taken me through physics, organic farming, CNC machining, electrical work, construction, and software. I enjoy learning how a system works and leaving it better than I found it.

I’m a father and will be traveling alone. Family is a central part of the stable future I’m working toward, not something I’m leaving behind.
