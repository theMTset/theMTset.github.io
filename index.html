I've always been driven by curiosity.

Before software: physics, then electrical work, then whatever the house needed next. A bathroom. A deck. A furnace two HVAC technicians had already given up on. I found what they missed.

Same question every time. How does this actually work?

Now the question points at interfaces and motion instead of wiring. I design the surface and build what runs underneath it — I've never been able to treat those as separate disciplines. Learn, understand, simplify, improve, ship.

The work above is the evidence.
